1. Identifying a Lease (3 Key Questions)

A contract contains a lease if the customer controls the use of an identified asset for a period of time in exchange for consideration. Tip: Select any text below to highlight it with yellow or green!

2. Lessee Accounting Model (Single Model)

At commencement date, lessee recognises a Right-of-Use (ROU) Asset and a Lease Liability.

Initial ROU Asset Value =
Initial Lease Liability (PV of unpaid lease payments)
+ Lease payments made at or before commencement (less lease incentives received)
+ Initial Direct Costs (IDC) incurred by lessee
+ Estimated dismantling / restoration cost (Ind AS 37)
